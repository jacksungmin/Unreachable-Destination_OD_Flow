Unreachable Destination OD Flow User Guide

This map summarizes survey-reported unmet transit needs across origin locations, destinations, and origin-destination connections.

These values are survey mentions of unmet transit needs. They should not be interpreted as observed trips, actual ridership, or measured travel volumes.

Key Terms

Origin
Where the transit user lives or starts their trip.
Destination
Where the transit user wants to go but does not have an available or adequate transit option.
OD Connection
A survey-reported connection from an origin to a desired destination.

Map Views

  • County Summary shows survey mentions summarized by origin county.
  • ZIP Summary shows survey mentions summarized by origin ZIP/ZCTA.
  • Destination Summary shows destination demand as bubbles.
  • Flowmap shows OD connections between origins and destinations.

Reading the Flowmap

  • Total needs shows total survey mentions associated with a location.
  • From this location shows needs starting from that origin location.
  • To this location shows needs pointing toward that destination.
  • OD connection lines show reported unmet transit connections.
  • Arrowheads point toward the desired destination.

Filters

  • Use Origin County to focus on where respondents start.
  • Use Destination Region to separate destinations inside or outside the 13-county region.
  • Use Advanced Demographic Filters to explore patterns by age, disability, income, or race.
  • Filters update the map and the destination chart.

Top Destinations

  • The panel ranks destinations by survey mentions from high to low.
  • Click a destination to filter the map to that destination.
  • Click the same destination again to clear that destination filter.
